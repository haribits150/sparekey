# User research outreach — WhatsApp draft

Send individually (not in a group) so answers stay honest. Replace [Name].

---

Hey [Name]! I'm working on a small side project to learn product management, called SpareKey, and I'd love your honest take.

The idea: when I travel, my place sits empty — meanwhile friends visiting town pay for hotels. SpareKey lets me offer my place to people I trust instead. No money changes hands, it's just friends helping friends, and it keeps score of the hotel money everyone saved.

Two quick questions:
1. If I were out of town and offered you my place instead of a hotel, would you take it?
2. Would you ever offer yours when you travel?

Most useful thing you can tell me: what would make you say *no*. Be brutal 😄

---

## Why this script works (research notes)
- **Positions it as a learning project**, not a startup pitch — friends give honest feedback to learners, polite feedback to founders.
- **Question 1 tests guest demand; question 2 tests host supply and the reciprocity loop.** You need both sides.
- **"What would make you say no"** beats "what do you think?" — it hunts disconfirming evidence, which is the whole point of user research.
- Follow-up if they say yes: "What would you need to see/know before saying yes to a 2-week stay?" (surfaces the real trust requirements).
